There is a version of leadership that announces itself loudly, that stakes its credibility on titles and metrics and the size of the stage. And then there is the version David McMahon learned on a football field in Westerville, Ohio, in a weight room at Butler University, and in the countless early mornings before anyone was keeping score. That version is quieter, more durable, and harder to fake. It starts with a commitment to the people around you. Everything else follows.

David (Dave to most people) is a Butler University MBA and a former Division I football player who captained the Butler Bulldogs for two seasons. He spent eleven years managing people, most recently as Vice President of Client Services at NetReputation, an Inc. 5000 company for six consecutive years. During his time there, the business grew from $5 million to $19 million in revenue. David built the operating infrastructure behind that growth, launched five new service lines, and led a 35-person global team across five countries through six directors. He did it in a flat organization, by building trust and accountability rather than leaning on authority.

Today David works as a fractional consultant in reputation management, SEO, branding, and AI implementation. He lives in Sarasota, Florida.

## Westerville, Ohio: football and rugby

David was born in Cleveland and raised in Westerville, outside Columbus. He is the middle child of three and the only boy between two sisters. He played baseball and football growing up, gave up baseball his junior year to focus on football, and then played rugby for the Westerville Worms in the spring of his senior year. The Worms won the Ohio and Midwest championships and reached the national final. More stories like this live in [Uncle Rico's Corner](/uncle-ricos-corner/).

## Butler football

David played football at Butler University, earning first-team All-Pioneer Football League honors as a defensive lineman in 2004, and was a team captain in his senior year and his fifth year. A defensive end, his playing career ended with a knee injury on October 7, 2006, a week after Butler beat Dayton, ending an 11-game losing streak in the series. The Butler Way, rooted in the legacy of coach Tony Hinkle and built on humility, passion, unity, servanthood, and thankfulness, became the standard he has carried into every role since.

## Indianapolis and the Butler MBA

David finished his MBA in Leadership at Butler in 2008 and stayed in Indianapolis. He lived in Broad Ripple, met his wife through neighbors in Broad Ripple Village, and spent two years doing advanced obedience training with his young German Shepherd, Emma. You can see Emma and their Valley Bulldog, Elsie, on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/emma_and_elsie/). Indianapolis felt a lot like Columbus, with NFL, NBA, and college basketball close by, the Monon Trail, the White River, and the Indianapolis Motor Speedway, which was a second home every May.

## Coaching and family

A career move brought David and his wife to Sarasota, where they are raising two kids. David coaches youth soccer and basketball. The same lessons apply to a team of seven-year-olds as to a team of directors: show up, be honest, and make the people around you better.

## Reputation management, SEO, and AI

David's reputation management work spans entity SEO, schema, and Google Knowledge Panels, content removal and promotion at volume, local search and review management, and tracking how brands appear in AI answers across Gemini, Google AI Mode, and ChatGPT. He made AI a default operating tool in 2023, always with governance and human judgment at the center. See the [resume](/resume/) for details, or [get in touch](/contact/).
